Welcome to RetroHof

Property maintenance without the chasing

Most property problems are not complicated. They are just tedious to organise — three quotes, two no-shows, and a job that still is not booked a fortnight later.

RetroHof exists to remove that. We are a multi-trade property maintenance company based in Hersham, working across southern England — from Hertfordshire in the north to Devon in the south west. Whether it is a single leaking tap, a full void turnaround before a new tenancy, or a commercial unit being handed back to a landlord, it is the same team, the same standard and the same point of contact.

  • Every trade under one roof, so nobody passes the job on
  • Written, itemised quotations before anything starts
  • Photographic records on every completed job
  • Direct tenant liaison with your authority

Our process

Four steps, no surprises

From the first phone call to the final walk-round, you always know what happens next.

  1. 01

    Tell us what needs doing

    Call, email or send the form. Photographs help. For urgent problems we will talk you through making things safe on the phone.

  2. 02

    Survey and fixed quote

    A site visit within a few working days, followed by a written, itemised quotation with a clear scope. No obligation, no charge.

  3. 03

    Booked in and programmed

    An agreed start date, an agreed working pattern, and a named contact who knows your property.

  4. 04

    Completed and signed off

    A joint walk-round, certificates handed over, site left clean, and twelve months of workmanship cover behind it.
